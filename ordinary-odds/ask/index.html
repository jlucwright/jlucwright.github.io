Ordinary Odds

Someone sent you an ordinary question.

Answer it for yourself before you read theirs.

Ordinary Odds asks eight questions about the frictions of your week, then restates each answer as what that friction needed in order to happen. No score and no comparison. Twenty-four further editions do compare you with sourced estimates, and show where every number came from. Answers stay on your iPhone. No account, no tracking, no public profile.
