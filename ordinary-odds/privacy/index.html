Privacy policy

Effective 26 August 2026

Ordinary Odds does not require an account and does not send your answers or portraits to us. They remain on your device unless you choose to share them.

Who is responsible

Studea Limited is the controller for personal information described in this policy. Contact us at hello@studea.co.uk.

Information stored by the app

Ordinary Odds stores answers, completed portraits, portrait history, your reference region, reminder settings and an onboarding flag in an App Group on your iPhone. The app uses this information to show your progress, let you revisit portraits, schedule reminders through iOS and supply the widget. It is not sent to Studea Limited.

Information we receive

The app does not send us your answers, portraits, name, email address, location, contacts, health information or advertising identifiers. It has no account system, answer sync, advertising SDK or cross-app tracking. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase identifier and purchase history for purchase validation and reporting.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any device or diagnostic details you choose to include. We use this information to answer your request and maintain the app. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in providing support. You do not have to contact support.

Purchases

Purchases and restores are processed by Apple through StoreKit. The app reads product, verified transaction and entitlement status to unlock Plus. RevenueCat receives anonymous purchase history to validate transactions and provide purchase reporting. Studea Limited and RevenueCat do not receive your payment-card details. Apple handles payment and App Store account information under its own privacy policy.

Service providers

RevenueCat processes anonymous purchase identifiers and purchase history for Studea Limited under its data-processing terms. Google Workspace processes support correspondence under its data-processing terms. These terms provide the applicable safeguards for transfers outside the UK. No service provider receives answers or portraits from the app.

Sharing

The app creates portrait images on your device. Nothing is shared automatically. When you press a share button, iOS shows its share sheet and you choose the destination. The privacy practices of that destination then apply.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you clear it from the app or delete the app. The Privacy screen removes saved answers, portraits, reminder settings and your reference region, and cancels Ordinary Odds reminders. It keeps the onboarding flag so the introduction is not shown again. Deleting the app removes its local data.

RevenueCat retains purchase records for purchase validation and reporting. You can ask us to delete the anonymous RevenueCat customer record by emailing support. Some transaction records may be retained where required for legal or fraud-prevention purposes.

We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to answer the request and deal with reasonable follow-up, or longer where needed for legal obligations or claims.

Your rights

For support correspondence, you may ask us to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal information. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interests. Some rights depend on the circumstances. Email hello@studea.co.uk to make a request.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated before the changed behaviour is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit Ordinary Odds support. Please do not include private answers or health information in an email.
